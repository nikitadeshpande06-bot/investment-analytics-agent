Market Analysis

Analyse the macro and sector environment relevant to the profile
  • Identify the current macro regime — growth/recession risk, inflation trend, rate environment
  • List 3–5 asset classes or sectors with tailwinds matching the time horizon and risk tolerance
  • Flag notable headwinds — rate sensitivity for bonds, valuation stretch in equities, etc.
  • Summarise in 3–5 concise bullet points — no fabricated statistics

Tailwind examples

  • Rate cuts → bond price appreciation
  • AI capex cycle → tech & infrastructure
  • Ageing demographics → healthcare
  • Energy transition → renewables/utilities
  • Emerging market growth → EM equities

Headwind examples

  • Elevated valuations → stretched P/E
  • Rising rates → duration risk in bonds
  • Geopolitical risk → commodity volatility
  • Credit tightening → small-cap stress
  • Stagflation → equity/bond correlation
3

Opportunity Evaluation

Apply fundamental and thematic analysis to each shortlisted asset

Fundamental Analysis

  • Valuation — P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA vs. history/peers
  • Earnings or income growth outlook
  • Debt level / balance sheet quality
  • Competitive moat or structural advantage

Technical / Thematic Overlay

  • Trend direction — bull / bear / neutral
  • Key thematic tailwinds (AI, demographics, energy)
  • Momentum and support levels (where relevant)

5

Action Plan

Concrete, prioritised steps for execution and ongoing management
1
Entry Strategy

Lump-sum vs. dollar-cost averaging (DCA). Recommend DCA intervals (weekly/monthly) when lump-sum timing risk is a concern or markets appear elevated.

2
Position Sizing

Order of execution — largest and most liquid positions first. Ensures core portfolio is established before smaller satellite positions.

3
Rebalancing

Threshold trigger: ±5% drift from target weight. Calendar schedule: semi-annual or annual review, whichever comes first.

4
Risk Management

Stop-loss levels, maximum drawdown rules, and tail-risk hedges tailored to the investor's risk tolerance and time horizon.

5
Monitoring Schedule

Monthly: macro/news scan. Quarterly: allocation drift check. Annual: full rebalance and goal reassessment.

6
Professional Referral

If tax optimisation, estate planning, or complex legal structures are relevant — recommend a qualified financial adviser or tax professional.

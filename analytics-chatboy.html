Singapore

📄

ADRs & GDRs

American/Global Depositary Receipts let you buy foreign companies (e.g. Infosys, Toyota) on your home exchange without opening a foreign brokerage account.

💱

Currency Risk

Investing abroad means returns move with exchange rates too. A strong home currency can reduce your foreign gains — diversify across currencies to soften this.

🕒

Trading Hours

Markets open at different local times (e.g. NYSE 9:30 ET, NSE 9:15 IST, LSE 8:00 GMT), so global investing gives you nearly round-the-clock opportunities.
